Privacy Policy

Mindkeep · Last updated 25 August 2026

The short version. Mindkeep stores everything on your phone, in an encrypted database. Your documents are read on your device. We do not have servers, we do not have your data, and there is no account to create.

What Mindkeep stores, and where

Everything you save — titles, notes, dates, categories, photographs of documents, and the text read from them — is stored locally on your device in a database encrypted at rest. Photographs are kept in Mindkeep's own private storage area, which other apps on your phone cannot read.

None of it is uploaded. There is no Mindkeep server to upload it to.

Reading your documents

When you photograph a document, the text is recognised entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning. The image is never sent anywhere for processing. Dates are then found in that text by ordinary pattern matching running on your phone — no artificial intelligence service is involved, and nothing leaves the device.

If Mindkeep later offers optional features that send content to an external service, they will be off by default, will name the service before anything is sent, and will require your explicit consent each time or until you say otherwise.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. They are not push notifications, nothing is sent from a server, and no one but your phone knows what you asked to be reminded about.

What we collect about you

Nothing. Specifically, Mindkeep contains:

  • no analytics or usage tracking;
  • no advertising, and no advertising identifiers;
  • no crash reporting that includes your content;
  • no account, login, email address, or profile.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Mindkeep Premium, the payment itself is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your installation and information about the purchase itself — what was bought and whether it is currently active. It does not receive your name, your email address, or any of your memories, documents, or reminders. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Your control over your data

  • Deleting a memory deletes its stored photograph too.
  • Deleting Mindkeep from your phone removes all of it — because all of it is on your phone.
  • Your existing memories and reminders keep working whether or not you subscribe. Nothing you saved is ever locked behind a payment.

Children

Mindkeep is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing and requires no account, there is nothing for us to hold in any case.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is handled, the updated policy will be published here with a new date, and material changes will be surfaced in the app.
